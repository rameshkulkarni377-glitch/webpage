PROJECT OVERVIEW

01

Sound Energy as a Power Source

Sound energy is a form of mechanical energy produced by vibrations and pressure variations in a medium. This project explores the possibility of utilizing this normally wasted energy as an alternative source of electricity.

02

Sound to Electrical Energy Conversion

Piezoelectric materials are used to capture mechanical vibrations caused by sound and convert them into a small electrical signal that can subsequently be conditioned and stored.

03

Sustainable Energy Generation

The concept can be explored in high-noise environments such as highways, industrial areas and airports, with the aim of generating sustainable energy for low-power applications.
